How FloodRoute Protects Hyderabad Commuters

From real-time citizen crowdsourcing to graph-based risk routing, our pipeline turns raw rainfall and hazard telemetry into actionable travel safety.

📍

1. Identify Hazards

Interactive Hyderabad GIS map plots verified citizen reports, sensor data, and chronic underpass bottlenecks in real time.

⚖️

2. Dynamic Risk Scoring

Multi-factor mathematical engine weights rainfall intensity, water depth, drainage vulnerability, and historical frequency.

🛣️

3. Safe Route Search

Dijkstra and A* graph algorithms calculate risk-penalized edge costs, providing safe alternative detours that avoid submerged points.

📢

4. Citizen Empowerment

Citizens upload geo-tagged photo reports. GHMC engineers verify submissions to trigger instant smart alerts.

GHMC Monsoon Commuter Safety Advisory

🚫 Turn Around, Don't Drown

Just 15 cm of moving water can stall a two-wheeler, while 30 cm can float small cars. Never attempt crossing a submerged underpass.

⚡ Electrical Hazard Caution

Stay clear of fallen electric poles, open feeder boxes, and cables touching standing water along flooded pedestrian pathways.
